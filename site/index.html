Claude Code · Cursor · VS Code · Codex

Connect in one step

In Claude Code, add the marketplace and install the plugin. It connects the server and teaches Claude how to record, edit, check its work and export. Other clients take one line of config, or a click in the app’s gear menu.

/plugin marketplace add smartloop-labs/screenforge-plugins
/plugin install screenforge@screenforge

Setup for every client

Record

“Record this window”

A whole display, or one window followed as it moves, with your microphone and system audio if you want them. Your agent can pause, resume and drop markers while you work.

Edit

Every tool the editor has

Cuts, speed, zooms, masks, titles, chapters, music, captions, narration and every look setting. It renders frames to check its own work, then exports with a title, description and share link.

One call

Hand over the whole job

“A 30-second vertical cut with narration and music.” With Pro, ScreenForge’s own editor does the looking and the cutting and reports back, so your agent spends few tokens.

Templates

Nothing to record? Still a video

Explainers, how-tos and announcements start from a template. Your agent writes the hook and the beats; ScreenForge lays them out, narrates and captions them.

The MCP server is in the macOS and Linux apps today. The agent built into the app works on Windows too.

In the app

Or ask inside the app.

The same kind of agent is built into the editor. Say what you want and watch the timeline change as it works, then fine-tune anything by hand. One ⌘Z undoes a whole answer.

The app

Record, edit, export.
One native app.

Everything your agent drives is yours to use by hand: a recorder and a timeline editor in one window. No Electron, no web view; the compositor runs on the GPU and the UI is drawn natively.

AI editing agent new

Edit by asking

Say what you want and watch the timeline change as the agent works. It reads the transcript, your clicks and the video itself, then cuts, zooms, speeds up, adds music or narration. Auto edit tightens the whole video; one ⌘Z undoes a whole answer.

Async video

Record, share a link, skip the meeting

Tick “Make a share link too” and Export uploads the video, then lists every way to paste it: the link, the MP4, Markdown for GitHub and Notion, and an embed for your site. It plays in any browser, no sign-in, and unfurls with a preview in chat. Export again and the same link plays the new version.

Zoom

Automatic zooms

Zooms open on your clicks and follow the cursor as you work, easing in and out. Adjust or remove any of them on the timeline.

Camera

You on camera

Overlay, split or full screen, switched along the timeline. The camera follows your face, and Resolve-style colour grading with LUTs makes it look right.

Captions

Captions from your voice

Transcribed on your computer with Whisper, nothing uploaded. Line by line or word by word, burned into the export or saved as SRT and WebVTT.

Voice-over

Narrate after you record

Press R and talk over the video, or type a script and an AI voice reads it: six voices on your computer, eight studio voices online, or your own voice cloned by ElevenLabs. Clean up a take or re-voice it, keeping your timing and accent.

Bring your videos

Import from Loom

Moving from Loom? Paste a Loom link and the video becomes a project you can edit. Any MP4 or MOV starts a project when you drop it, or joins the timeline of an open one.

Privacy and framing

Blur, highlight, crop

Blur passwords and private details or dim everything but the part that matters, timed on the timeline. Crop to the part of the screen you need; zooms and cursor follow.

Native and fast

GPU rendering, native capture

ScreenCaptureKit, Desktop Duplication or PipeWire capture; a wgpu compositor renders the timeline. Export MP4, HEVC, ProRes, GIF or WebM with mixed audio.

Devices and keys

iPhone frames, shortcuts on screen

Record an iPhone or iPad over a cable and show it in a device frame. Shortcuts you press appear as key caps, like ⇧ ⌘ S, so viewers can follow along.

Looks and sound

Backgrounds, frames and music

Set the recording on a wallpaper, your desktop's own, a gradient or an Unsplash photo, with shadow and inset. Put a phone video in a device frame. Pick a track from the built-in music library, lo-fi to cinematic, or drop in your own.
